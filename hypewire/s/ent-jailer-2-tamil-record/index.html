Jailer 2 becomes the highest-grossing Tamil film of all time

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 passed about ₹710 crore worldwide in 11 days, beating 2.0's record.

By the numbers

₹710 cr+worldwide gross
₹691 crprevious record, 2.0
11days to the record

What happened

Jailer 2 is the first Tamil film past ₹700 crore, ahead of 2.0's ₹691 crore, One Mann's Movies reports, citing Sacnilk. Sacnilk says it also became the best-selling Tamil film on BookMyShow, with 92 lakh tickets.

Why it matters

Rajinikanth is one of India's biggest stars, and Tamil cinema rarely reaches box office numbers this size.

Why the hype

  • Big nameRajinikanth has led Tamil cinema for decades.
  • ScaleIt is the first Tamil film to pass ₹700 crore worldwide.
  • SpeedIt set the record in just 11 days.

Background

Tamil cinema is the film industry of the Indian state of Tamil Nadu. Jailer (2023) was a major hit for Rajinikanth.

What to watch next

Watch for whether it keeps climbing in its third week and its final overseas total.

The take
