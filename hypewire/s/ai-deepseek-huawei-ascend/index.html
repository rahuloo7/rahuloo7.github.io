DeepSeek is reported to plan training on Huawei chips by late 2026

The Information reports DeepSeek will pre-train a model on Huawei's Ascend 950DT, not Nvidia hardware.

By the numbers

Q4 2026Ascend 950DT shipping, reported

What happened

According to The Information, DeepSeek is helping shape Huawei's AI chips and plans to pre-train on the Ascend 950DT by late 2026. If accurate, it would be the first flagship Chinese model trained on domestic chips. The 950DT is said to ship with Huawei's own HBM in the fourth quarter.

Why it matters

A top Chinese model trained on Chinese chips would be a milestone for China's push to rely less on Nvidia.

Why the hype

  • RivalryIt would test whether Chinese chips can replace Nvidia's for top models.
  • RiskUS export limits make access to Nvidia chips uncertain for Chinese labs.
  • Big nameDeepSeek's low-cost models made it China's best-known AI lab.

Background

DeepSeek is a Chinese AI lab known for low-cost models. Huawei makes the Ascend line of AI chips.

What to watch next

Watch for confirmation from DeepSeek or Huawei and any model trained on the new chips.
