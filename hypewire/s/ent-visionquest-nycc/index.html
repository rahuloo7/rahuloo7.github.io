Marvel's VisionQuest premieres on Disney+ to mostly positive reviews

Paul Bettany returns as Vision, with James Spader's Ultron, in an eight-episode Disney+ series.

By the numbers

8episodes

What happened

VisionQuest premiered on Disney+ this week with mostly positive reviews, ComicBookMovie reports, citing a Fresh Rotten Tomatoes score at the review embargo. Critics praised Bettany and Spader, while some said the season spends too long on backstory. Terry Matalas co-wrote the series.

Why it matters

VisionQuest continues a story Marvel fans have followed since WandaVision.

Why the hype

  • Fan demandMarvel series still draw some of streaming's biggest audiences.
