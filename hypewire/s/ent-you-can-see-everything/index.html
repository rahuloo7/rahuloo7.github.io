You Can See Everything rises in its second weekend to $19.5 million

The A24 film grew about a quarter from its opening weekend and finished second behind Clayface.

By the numbers

$19.5Msecond weekend
+25%vs. opening weekend
$45.1Mdomestic total

What happened

The Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim film about Elizabeth Holmes rose 25% to $19.5 million in North America, One Mann's Movies reports. Boxoffice Pro puts its domestic total at $45.1 million.

Why it matters

Films rarely grow in week two, so it is a strong sign of word of mouth for a documentary-style release.

Why the hype

  • TrendGrowing in week two is rare and points to strong word of mouth.
  • RivalryIt is running a close second to DC's Clayface.
  • Big nameNathan Fielder has a devoted following from his TV work.

Background

Elizabeth Holmes founded the blood-testing company Theranos and was convicted of fraud in 2022.

What to watch next

Watch for whether it holds up over Halloween weekend.
