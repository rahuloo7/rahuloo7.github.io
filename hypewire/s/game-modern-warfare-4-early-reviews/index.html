Modern Warfare 4 launches to strong reviews and some matchmaking gripes

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is out on PS5, Xbox, PC and Switch 2, with an early Metacritic score of 84 on PC.

By the numbers

84Metacritic, PC, early
77OpenCritic, early
$69.99standard price

What happened

Early scores include 90 from Destructoid and 8.5 from Game Informer, GameDaily reports. Critics praise the campaign finale and DMZ, while some cite long queues, uneven maps and slow framerates in busy scenes. It costs $69.99 and skipped Game Pass.

Why it matters

Call of Duty is one of the best-selling series in games, and this is its first launch on a Nintendo console in years.

Why the hype

  • ScaleCall of Duty is usually among the year's best-selling games in the US.
  • First of its kindIt is the first Modern Warfare game on a Switch console.
  • RivalryIt no longer launches into Game Pass, after Xbox removed new Call of Duty games.

Background

Call of Duty is Activision's shooter series, now owned by Microsoft. Infinity Ward made this game.

What to watch next

Watch for console Metascores and the first sales figures.
