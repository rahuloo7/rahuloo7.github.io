Modern Warfare 4 early access gets 'Very Positive' Steam reviews

About 86% of the first 932 Steam reviews recommend Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, with the Switch 2 version due October 23.

By the numbers

What happened

Gaming Route reports 800 of 932 Steam reviews recommend the game, a small early-access sample. The Switch 2 version launches October 23 for $69.99 and uses DLSS or SMAA upscaling.

Why it matters

Call of Duty is gaming's biggest annual series, and early player reaction shapes its launch.

Why the hype

  • ScaleCall of Duty sells millions of copies every year.
  • First of its kindThe Switch 2 version brings the series back to Nintendo hardware.
  • TimingIt launches weeks before GTA VI.

Background

Call of Duty is Activision's shooter series, now owned by Microsoft.

What to watch next

Watch for whether review scores hold once the full player base arrives.
