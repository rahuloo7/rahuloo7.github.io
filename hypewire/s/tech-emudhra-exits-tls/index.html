eMudhra quits the web certificate business after its keys proved guessable

The Indian certificate authority is leaving the global SSL/TLS market after keys for eight certificates were recovered.

By the numbers

8intermediate CA keys recovered
~Oct 27Chrome distrust date

What happened

A researcher recovered private keys for eight of eMudhra's intermediate CA certificates, The Hacker News reports. Mozilla dropped trust for Firefox on October 20, and Chrome plans to follow around October 27.

Why it matters

Certificate authorities underpin the padlock in your browser, so a weak one is a real risk.

Why the hype

  • RiskGuessable keys could let someone fake a secure website.
  • First of its kindIt is rare for a certificate authority to exit the market over such a flaw.

Background

Certificate authorities issue the digital certificates that browsers use to confirm a website is genuine.

What to watch next

Watch for Chrome's change and how sites using eMudhra certificates switch providers.

The take
