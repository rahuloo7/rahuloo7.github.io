Chip stocks bounce after their longest losing run in 16 years

Chipmakers rose on Monday after the semiconductor index fell 11% last week over doubts about AI spending.

By the numbers

-11%chip index last week
8straight down days

What happened

The PHLX Semiconductor Index fell 11% last week as chip shares dropped eight days in a row, the longest such run in 16 years, Schwab notes. On Monday, Intel rose about 3% after a JPMorgan upgrade, with Marvell and SK Hynix also up early.

Why it matters

Chip stocks have led the AI rally, so their swings move the whole market.

Why the hype

  • MoneyChip stocks make up a large share of major indexes.
  • RiskDoubts about AI spending triggered the selloff.
  • TimingBig tech earnings start this week.

Background

The PHLX Semiconductor Index tracks major US-listed chipmakers, including Nvidia, AMD and Intel.

What to watch next

Watch for whether the rebound holds through this week's big tech earnings reports.
