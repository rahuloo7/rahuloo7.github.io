US lends up to $4.4 billion to restart three nuclear plants for AI power

The Energy Department is backing restarts at Palisades, Crane and Duane Arnold, restoring about 2.25 gigawatts mainly for data centers.

By the numbers

$4.4Bfederal loans, up to
2.25 GWcapacity restored
3plants restarting

What happened

Crane, the former Three Mile Island plant, has a 20-year deal with Microsoft and a 2027 target. Palisades in Michigan must be online by March 2027 under its contracts. Duane Arnold in Iowa, owned by NextEra and working with Google, aims for early 2029.

Why it matters

AI data centers need huge amounts of power, and reviving shut reactors is one of the quickest ways to find it.

Why the hype

  • Scale2.25 gigawatts is enough for about 2 million homes and businesses.
  • Big nameMicrosoft and Google are tied to two of the plants.
  • TrendTech companies are turning to nuclear power for AI.

Background

Nuclear plants produce steady power around the clock, which data centers need.

What to watch next

Watch for Palisades finishing fuel loading after a pause in its restart.
