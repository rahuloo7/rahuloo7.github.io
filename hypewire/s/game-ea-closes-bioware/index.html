EA is closing BioWare and cancelling the next Mass Effect

The studio behind Mass Effect and Dragon Age is shutting as EA cuts about 400 jobs after its $55 billion buyout.

By the numbers

~400EA jobs cut, reported
$55BEA buyout

What happened

BioWare said early work on the next Mass Effect is ending, and Bloomberg reports about 400 EA jobs are going. A Canadian party leader has since called for federal support so staff could take over the studio, This Week in Videogames reports.

Why it matters

BioWare made some of the most loved role-playing games of the last 30 years.

Why the hype

  • NostalgiaBioWare made Baldur's Gate, Knights of the Old Republic and Mass Effect.
  • MoneyThe buyout added about $18 billion in debt, and EA has promised big savings.
  • ScaleAbout 400 jobs are going across EA, Bloomberg reports.

Background

BioWare was founded in 1995 in Edmonton and bought by EA in 2007. Its last game was Dragon Age: The Veilguard in 2024.

What to watch next

Watch for what EA does with the Mass Effect and Dragon Age names and news on the TV series.
