Meta releases Muse Wonder, pitching it as a rival to ChatGPT and Claude

Meta's new Muse Wonder models are pitched against OpenAI and Anthropic on coding, reasoning and long agent tasks.

By the numbers

56Artificial Analysis index score
$4per 1M input tokens, reported

What happened

Meta launched Muse Wonder and a larger Muse Wonder Full on October 12. Artificial Analysis scored Full at 56 on its Intelligence Index, second behind Claude Opus 5.5, AI Weekly reports. API prices are reported at about $4 and $20 per million tokens.

Why it matters

It is Meta's clearest attempt yet to rejoin the race for the most capable AI models.

Why the hype

  • RivalryMeta is pitching the models directly against OpenAI and Anthropic.
  • MoneyMeta spent about $14 billion on a Scale AI deal that brought in its AI chief.
  • TimingIt follows about 14 months of restructuring in Meta's AI unit.

Background

Meta owns Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp. Its earlier Llama models were released with open weights.

What to watch next

Watch for how Muse Wonder compares with Google's Gemini 4 Argon in independent tests.
