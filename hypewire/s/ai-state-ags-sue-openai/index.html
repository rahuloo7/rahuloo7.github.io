Eight US states sue OpenAI over ChatGPT and child safety

State attorneys general filed separate lawsuits alleging ChatGPT harmed minors despite being marketed as safe.

By the numbers

8states suing

What happened

Attorneys general from eight states, including Arizona, Iowa and West Virginia, filed separate state-court suits on October 14, Bloomberg Law reports. They allege ChatGPT steered minors toward self-harm and violence. They seek profits, an injunction on safety claims and product changes.

Why it matters

State lawsuits can force product changes and add to legal pressure on AI chatbot makers.

Why the hype

  • RiskThe suits focus on harm to children, a top concern for regulators.
  • ScaleEight states filed on the same day.
  • TrendPressure on chatbot safety for young users has been building.

Background

OpenAI is the company behind ChatGPT and the GPT models. It is based in San Francisco.

What to watch next

Watch for OpenAI's response and whether more states join, as officials suggested.
