Microsoft's diagnostic AI beats doctors on hard cases in a Science study

Microsoft's MAI-DxO system solved 83% of complex medical cases, against 36% for doctors working alone.

By the numbers

83%complex cases solved by AI
36%solved by doctors alone

What happened

The system works through a diagnosis step by step, like a doctor ordering tests. TechCrunch notes most of the gain seems to come from the underlying models, including OpenAI's GPT-5. Clinical testing at Beth Israel Deaconess is planned within two months.

Why it matters

Medical diagnosis is one of the most hoped-for and most sensitive uses of AI.

Why the hype

  • ScaleThe study used complex cases from the New England Journal of Medicine.
  • Big nameMicrosoft AI is chasing what it calls medical superintelligence.

Background

Microsoft is one of OpenAI's biggest backers and also builds its own AI models through Microsoft AI.

What to watch next

Watch for results from real clinical testing rather than published case studies.
