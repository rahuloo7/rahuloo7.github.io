Tencent launches Hy4 Flash, a fast model priced at seven cents per million tokens

Tencent's Hy4 Flash has 224 billion parameters, uses 12 billion per request and handles up to a million tokens at very low prices.

By the numbers

$0.07per M input tokens
224Btotal parameters
1Mtokens of context

What happened

Tencent says Hy4 Flash is multimodal, with up to 1 million tokens of context. It costs about $0.07 per million input tokens and $0.281 per million output tokens. Tencent says it costs 24.5 times less per task than Hy4.

Why it matters

Chinese labs keep pushing AI prices down, which pressures every rival on cost.

Why the hype

  • MoneyInput costs about $0.07 per million tokens.
  • RivalryChinese labs are competing hard on price with US models.

Background

Tencent is a Chinese tech giant that owns WeChat and a large games business.

What to watch next

Watch for independent tests of its speed and quality against other cheap models.
