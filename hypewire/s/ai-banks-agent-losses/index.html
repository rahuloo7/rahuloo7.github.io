Citi, PNC and Citizens say customers carry the losses from third-party AI agents

Three US banks changed their terms so customers are responsible for losses caused by outside AI agents with account access, even if hacked.

By the numbers

3banks updating terms
Octwhen JPMorgan and BofA moved first

What happened

TechCrunch reports the new terms apply even when the AI agent itself is compromised. JPMorgan and Bank of America made similar changes in October. Citi exempts its own AI agents from the rule, AI Weekly reports.

Why it matters

AI agents that move money are being pitched widely, and this decides who pays when one goes wrong.

Why the hype

  • RiskCustomers are liable even if the agent is hacked.
  • TrendFive big US banks have now made similar changes.
  • MoneyIt shifts the cost of agent mistakes onto account holders.

Background

AI agents are programs that can act for a user, such as logging in to accounts and making payments.

What to watch next

Watch for whether other banks follow and whether consumer regulators weigh in.
