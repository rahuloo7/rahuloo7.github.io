Oppo launches the Find X10 phones with up to three 200MP cameras

The Find X10 Pro Max has three 200MP sensors and a 7,150 mAh battery, starting at about EUR 1,799 in Spain.

By the numbers

3 x 200MPPro Max cameras
7,150 mAhPro Max battery
EUR 1,399starting price, Spain

What happened

Oppo launched the Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max internationally, Cinco Dias reports. The standard model has two 200MP cameras and the Pro Max three. Both run ColorOS 17 on Android 17, with Spanish prices from about EUR 1,399 and EUR 1,799.

Why it matters

Phone makers are racing on camera specs, and this is one of the year's most loaded flagships.
