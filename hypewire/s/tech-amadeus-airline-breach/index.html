Amadeus flaw exposed booking data from several Asian airlines

Travel tech firm Amadeus fixed a flaw that allowed access to airline booking data, with Japan Airlines, ANA and Cathay Pacific reporting affected customers.

By the numbers

~92,000JAL group customers affected
~53,000Cathay bookings affected
~37,800ANA passengers, reported

What happened

Japan Airlines reported about 92,000 affected customers, ANA about 37,800 passengers and Cathay about 53,000 bookings, IBTimes says. Amadeus said there is no evidence payment data was accessed. Korean Air and Asiana also issued alerts.

Why it matters

Amadeus runs booking systems for much of the world's airline industry.

Why the hype

  • ScaleSeveral major airlines have reported affected customers.
  • RiskExposed names and booking details can be used in phishing messages.

Background

Amadeus is a Spanish travel technology company whose systems handle bookings for many airlines and travel agents.

What to watch next

Watch for more airlines confirming whether their passengers were affected.

The take
