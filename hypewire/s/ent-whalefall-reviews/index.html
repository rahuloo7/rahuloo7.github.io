Whalefall, about a diver swallowed by a whale, opens to solid reviews

Brian Duffield's survival thriller opens October 16 with 79% on Rotten Tomatoes.

What happened

Whalefall stars Austin Abrams as a diver swallowed by a sperm whale with an hour of oxygen, Dark Horizons reports. It holds 79% on Rotten Tomatoes from 43 reviews and 64 on Metacritic. It is based on Daniel Kraus's novel and is from 20th Century Studios.

Why it matters

An original-feeling thriller is a rare draw in a weekend led by a game adaptation.
