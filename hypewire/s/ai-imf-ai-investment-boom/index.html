IMF raises its 2026 growth forecast and credits the AI investment boom

The IMF now sees 3.1% global growth this year and modeled the cost of a sudden pullback in AI spending.

By the numbers

3.1%2026 global growth forecast
-1.1%GDP if AI spending reverses

What happened

The IMF raised its 2026 global growth forecast to 3.1%, citing the AI investment boom, ABC News reports. It also modeled a downside case in which a reversal in AI spending leaves world output 1.1% below its baseline by 2028.

Why it matters

It shows how much the world economy now leans on spending for AI data centers and chips.
