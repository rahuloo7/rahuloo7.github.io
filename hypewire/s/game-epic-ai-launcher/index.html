Epic tests a new launcher it says was built entirely with AI

Epic's rebuilt Epic Games Launcher is in testing and, the company claims, boots faster than Steam.

By the numbers

~5.5scold start, per Epic
10s+Steam cold start, per Epic

What happened

Epic says the new launcher cold-starts to the store in about 5.5 seconds, versus just over 10 for Steam, and is in testing on Windows, Mac and, for the first time, Linux. Its post explained the use of AI tools by saying programming is hard.

Why it matters

It is a high-profile test of AI-written software, from a company that has openly pivoted to AI development.

Why the hype

  • TrendEpic's CEO announced a company-wide shift to AI-accelerated development.
  • RivalryEpic compared the launcher directly with Valve's Steam.
  • ControversyEpic laid off about 1,000 staff in March.

Background

Epic Games makes Fortnite and the Unreal Engine and runs the Epic Games Store.

What to watch next

Watch for player tests of the speed claims and when the new launcher rolls out widely.
