I Play Rocky takes second with $9.3 million in its wide expansion

Peter Farrelly's Stallone biopic expanded from limited release to earn $9.3 million and finish second, Flickering Myth reports.

By the numbers

$9.3Mweekend gross
$15-20Mpre-weekend forecast
98%audience score

What happened

That fell short of BoxOfficePro's $15 million to $20 million forecast for the weekend. The film went in with a 98% audience score and an 87% critic score.

Why it matters

A crowd-pleasing film about making Rocky could become counter-programming to the Hunger Games prequel.
