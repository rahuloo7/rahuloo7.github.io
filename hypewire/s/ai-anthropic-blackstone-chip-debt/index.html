Anthropic is reported to offer Blackstone a 10% yield on chip debt

The FT reports Anthropic offered over 10% on a $9 billion slice of a $60 billion debt package to pay for Google TPU chips.

By the numbers

What happened

The Financial Times reported that Anthropic offered Blackstone a yield above 10% on a $9 billion tranche of $60 billion in TPU debt arranged by Broadcom. The FT also said Anthropic was rated BB+, the top junk grade, in June.

Why it matters

It shows how much borrowing now sits behind the AI build-out, and what lenders want in return.

TPUs are Google's AI chips. Broadcom helps design and supply them.
