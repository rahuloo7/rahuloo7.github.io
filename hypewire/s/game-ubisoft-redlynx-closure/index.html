Ubisoft proposes closing Trials studio RedLynx and cuts 70 roles in Singapore

The latest round of Ubisoft's restructuring could close the Finnish studio and trims the Skull and Bones team.

By the numbers

52RedLynx positions affected
70Ubisoft Singapore roles cut

What happened

Ubisoft proposed closing RedLynx, affecting up to 52 positions, subject to local talks. It also cut 70 roles at Ubisoft Singapore, and separately proposed voluntary departures at its Anno and Settlers studios in Germany.

Why it matters

It extends a long run of cuts at one of Europe's biggest game publishers.

Why the hype

  • TrendUbisoft's reset has already cancelled six games and delayed seven.
  • NostalgiaRedLynx made the Trials motorbike series.
  • ScaleCuts span Finland, Singapore and Germany in one week.

Background

Ubisoft is the French publisher behind Assassin's Creed and Far Cry. It bought RedLynx in 2011.

What to watch next

Watch for the outcome of negotiations in Finland and further changes to Ubisoft's studios.
