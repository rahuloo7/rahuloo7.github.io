Recursive releases Flint, a small open coding model built by an automated lab

Flint has about 40 billion parameters, uses 2.5 billion per token and is free under Apache 2.0.

By the numbers

89.7%SWE-bench Pro v2, reported
~40Btotal parameters
2.5Bactive per token

What happened

Recursive, Richard Socher's startup, says Flint was produced by its automated AI research system. AI Weekly reports 89.7% on SWE-bench Pro v2. Socher said it can top 125 tokens per second on a MacBook Pro.

Why it matters

A strong coding model that runs on a laptop could cut costs for developers.

Why the hype

  • ScaleIt activates only about 2.5 billion parameters per token, small enough for a laptop.
  • TrendOpen coding models are closing in on paid ones for everyday tasks.

Background

Richard Socher is an AI researcher who founded the search engine You.com.

What to watch next

Watch for independent tests of Flint's benchmark scores and how developers rate it in real use.
