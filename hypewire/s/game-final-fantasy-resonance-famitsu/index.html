Final Fantasy Resonance gets 36 out of 40 in its first review

Famitsu's reviewers scored Square Enix's new RPG highly ahead of its October 22 release.

What happened

Famitsu's four reviewers each gave Final Fantasy Resonance 9 out of 10, Pure Xbox reports. Famitsu puts the main story at about 20 to 30 hours and full completion near 70. It launches October 22 at $49.99 on Xbox and PC, with other platforms listed.
