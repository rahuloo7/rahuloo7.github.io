DeepSeek is reported to be raising about $15 billion

China's DeepSeek is reported to be closing a round of about 100 billion yuan, double its original target.

By the numbers

~$15Bround size, reported
~$75Bpre-money value, reported
2 daysafter US federal ban

What happened

Bloomberg and Reuters report DeepSeek is wrapping up a round of about $15 billion, with CATL and Tencent among reported investors. Reuters put the pre-money valuation near $75 billion. It comes two days after the US ordered DeepSeek out of federal civilian systems.

Why it matters

It would be one of China's largest AI raises and gives a rival to US labs far more money to spend.

Why the hype

  • MoneyThe round would be about double DeepSeek's original 50 billion yuan target, Reuters reports.
  • RivalryDeepSeek is China's best-known challenger to US labs such as OpenAI and Anthropic.
  • TimingThe news landed two days after a US order to remove its AI from federal systems.

Background

DeepSeek is an AI lab based in Hangzhou, China. Its open-weight R1 model drew global attention in early 2025.

What to watch next

Watch for DeepSeek confirming the round, its final investors, and any talk of a listing in China.
