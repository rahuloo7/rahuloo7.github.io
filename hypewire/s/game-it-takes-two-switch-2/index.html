It Takes Two comes to Switch 2 on October 15 with cross-play

EA's co-op hit gets a free upgrade for Switch owners, with 1080p and 60fps when docked.

By the numbers

Oct 15Switch 2 release
60fpsdocked frame rate

What happened

It Takes Two arrives on Switch 2 on October 15 as a free upgrade for Switch 1 owners, Nintendo Life reports. It runs at 1080p and 60fps docked and supports cross-play with PC, Xbox and PlayStation. The Friend's Pass stays.

Why it matters

It Takes Two is one of the most popular co-op games, and cross-play lets friends on other consoles join.

Why the hype

  • Fan demandCo-op fans have asked for wider cross-play.
  • TimingIt lands in a busy week of Switch 2 releases.

Background

It Takes Two is a two-player co-op adventure from Hazelight Studios, published by Electronic Arts in 2021.
