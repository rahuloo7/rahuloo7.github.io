Other Mommy opens at No. 1 with $27.5 million

The Jessica Chastain horror film took $27.5 million on a $19 million budget despite a D+ audience grade.

By the numbers

$27.5Mopening weekend
$45Mworldwide opening
D+CinemaScore

What happened

Universal and Blumhouse's Other Mommy opened to $27.5 million and $45 million worldwide, TheWrap reports. Its D+ CinemaScore is the lowest since Megalopolis. Verity fell 64% to about $11.5 million in its second weekend, and Resident Evil was third.

Why it matters

October is horror season, and an original film leading the chart is a useful sign for cinemas.

Why the hype

  • TimingA horror release in October has the calendar on its side.
  • MoneyIt took more than its $19 million budget in three days.
  • ControversyA D+ CinemaScore means opening-night audiences did not like it.

Background

Blumhouse is a production company known for low-budget horror hits. Universal is one of Hollywood's major studios.

What to watch next

Watch for how far it falls this weekend against Street Fighter, given its weak audience score.
