NSA's classified network goes down in a week of US tech outages

The NSA's classified internal network went down, adding to outages at Microsoft, Apple, banks and the FAA; officials say there is no sign of an attack.

What happened

DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin blamed new technology layered on old systems, Reuters reports. The FAA paused installs of a Juniper router type linked to last Friday's ground stop. Politico says lawmakers have asked for explanations.

Why it matters

A string of failures at critical services has made people uneasy, and lawmakers want answers.
