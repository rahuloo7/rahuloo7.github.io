DeepSeek releases an open V4.1 Flash model with built-in web search

DeepSeek posted V4.1-Flash-1017 on Hugging Face under an MIT license, adding live search and page fetching.

By the numbers

552Btotal parameters
1Mtoken context window
$0.75per 1,000 search requests

What happened

The model is a sparse mixture-of-experts with 552 billion total parameters and a one-million-token context window. DeepSeek's search and fetch tools cost $0.75 per 1,000 successful requests, according to its Hugging Face page.

Why it matters

DeepSeek's cheap open models keep pressure on US labs' prices.

Why the hype

  • RivalryChinese open models compete directly with US paid services.
  • MoneyLow prices are DeepSeek's main selling point.

Background

DeepSeek is a Chinese AI lab whose low-cost models drew wide attention in early 2025.

What to watch next

Watch for independent benchmark tests and how developers compare it with US models.
