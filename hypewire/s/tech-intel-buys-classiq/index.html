Intel is reported to buy quantum software firm Classiq for about $2.2 billion

Intel agreed to buy Tel Aviv-based Classiq, its first major acquisition in four years, Israeli media report.

What happened

Globes and Calcalist put the price at about $2.2 billion to $2.3 billion. Classiq has raised over $200 million and employs about 130 people. Closing is targeted for the first half of 2027.

Why it matters

It marks a new push into quantum computing by a chipmaker trying to rebuild its position.
