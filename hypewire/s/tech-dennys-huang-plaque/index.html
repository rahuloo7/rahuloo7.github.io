Denny's restores Jensen Huang's plaque after his return to Nvidia

Denny's posted on X that it had restored a commemorative plaque to Nvidia's CEO and invited him back to his booth.

What happened

Tom's Hardware reports Denny's posted a roughly 200-word statement after Huang was reinstated. It says management put his plaque back after it had been removed and invited him to return to his booth.

Why it matters

The diner chain linked to Nvidia's founding turned a boardroom saga into a brand moment.

Why the hype

  • NostalgiaNvidia's founders famously planned the company at a Denny's.
  • Big nameHuang's return as CEO has been one of the week's biggest tech stories.

Background

Nvidia's founders planned the company at a Denny's restaurant in San Jose in 1993.

What to watch next

Watch for whether Huang takes up the invitation.
