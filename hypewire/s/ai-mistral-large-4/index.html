Mistral unveils Large 4, a trillion-parameter model trained in Europe

The French lab's new multimodal model is in public preview, with open weights promised by the end of October.

By the numbers

1Tparameters
52Bactive parameters
$1.36per million input tokens

What happened

Mistral Large 4 is a mixture-of-experts model with 1 trillion parameters, 52 billion active, trained on 3,800 Nvidia Grace Blackwell GPUs in European data centres. Preview pricing is $1.36 per million input tokens and $4.18 per million output.

Why it matters

Europe's best-known AI lab is trying to keep pace with US and Chinese frontier models.

Why the hype

  • ScaleIt has 1 trillion parameters, with 52 billion used for each token.
  • OpennessMistral says the weights will be released by the end of October.
  • RivalryFew European labs compete with US and Chinese firms at the frontier.

Background

Mistral AI is a Paris-based AI company founded in 2023 that releases many of its models with open weights.

What to watch next

Watch for the weights release at the end of October and independent benchmark results.
