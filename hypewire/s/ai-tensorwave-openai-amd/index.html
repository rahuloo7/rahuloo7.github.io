OpenAI signs a six-year, 457-megawatt AMD chip deal with TensorWave

TensorWave will deploy 457 megawatts of AMD Instinct MI455X and Helios systems for OpenAI over six years, starting in early 2027.

By the numbers

457 MWAMD capacity for OpenAI
6 yrslength of the agreement
2+ GWTensorWave long-term capacity

What happened

Capacity starts coming online in the first half of 2027 and is due to be fully deployed by early 2028. TensorWave says it now has more than 2 gigawatts of long-term data center capacity.

Why it matters

OpenAI keeps spreading its compute beyond Nvidia, and every deal like this is a win for AMD.

Why the hype

  • TimingCapacity arrives from the first half of 2027 through early 2028.
  • RivalryIt is another large AI deal that runs on AMD rather than Nvidia.

Background

AMD is Nvidia's main rival in AI data center chips. TensorWave is a cloud provider built on AMD hardware.

What to watch next

Watch for whether the first capacity arrives on time in 2027.
