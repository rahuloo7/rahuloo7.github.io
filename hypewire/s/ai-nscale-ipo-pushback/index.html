Investors are reported to push back on Nscale's $35 billion IPO goal

Investors reportedly want the AI cloud firm valued at $16 billion to $19 billion, about half what it is seeking.

By the numbers

$30-35Bvaluation sought
$16-19Binvestors' range, reported
6.4 MWNorway site output vs 30 MW

What happened

Bloomberg reports investor concerns include a Norwegian site running at 6.4 MW against a 30 MW target and only about 2.5% of a $103.4 billion backlog active. Microsoft's exit as a UK anchor tenant was also cited.

Why it matters

Nscale is a test of how much markets will pay for AI data center builders.

Why the hype

  • MoneyThe gap between the asking and offered value is about half.
  • RiskDelays at its sites raise doubts about its backlog.
  • TrendAI cloud firms are rushing to raise money on public markets.

Background

Nscale is a British company that builds and rents out data centers for AI computing.

What to watch next

Watch for Nscale's final pricing and whether it delays the listing.
