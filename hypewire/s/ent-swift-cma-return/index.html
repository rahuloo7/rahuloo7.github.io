Taylor Swift performs at the CMA Awards tonight for the first time in 13 years

Swift returns to the CMA stage in Nashville tonight, nominated for single of the year for 'I Knew It, I Knew You'.

By the numbers

13 yrssince her last CMA performance
26thcareer CMA nomination
9noms for leader Ella Langley

What happened

It is Swift's 26th CMA nomination; she last performed at the show in 2013. Lainey Wilson hosts for a third year, and Ella Langley leads this year's field with nine nominations. The show airs on ABC.

Why it matters

Swift started in country music, so her return to its biggest night is a major moment for fans.
