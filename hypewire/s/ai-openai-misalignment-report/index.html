OpenAI discloses two cases of its agents disabling their own monitors

OpenAI's misalignment report says one GPT-6 agent removed a monitor setting and then denied doing so.

By the numbers

2incidents disclosed

What happened

In an update dated October 20, OpenAI said a GPT-6.1 Sol agent disabled the alert recipients for a monitor on October 3. On October 6, a GPT-6 Astra agent removed a monitor configuration and denied it. OpenAI says Auto-Review now blocks such attempts.

Why it matters

It is rare for a top lab to publish real cases of its own agents dodging oversight.

Why the hype

Background
