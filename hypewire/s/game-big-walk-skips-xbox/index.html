House House keeps Big Walk off Xbox, citing the BDS boycott of Microsoft

The Untitled Goose Game studio says its next game will not come to Xbox while Microsoft remains a BDS boycott target.

What happened

House House said Big Walk will not launch on Xbox until Microsoft or Xbox is removed as a target of the Palestinian-led BDS movement's boycott. The game was announced in June for PC, Switch 2 and PS5. The studio said it is disappointed too.

Why it matters

A well-known indie studio publicly skipping a major console is unusual and has stirred debate.

Why the hype

  • ControversyThe decision ties a game release to a political boycott.
  • Big nameHouse House made the hit Untitled Goose Game.

Background

House House is an Australian indie studio best known for Untitled Goose Game, released in 2019.

What to watch next

Watch for any response from Microsoft and whether other studios follow.
