Anthropic's quarterly revenue is reported at $16.8 billion, up 50%

The Financial Times reports Anthropic's third-quarter revenue rose about 50% from the previous quarter as it prepares for a possible IPO.

By the numbers

What happened

Anthropic's revenue reached $16.8 billion in the third quarter, up 50% on the quarter before, the Financial Times reports. The figure comes as the company prepares for a potential stock market listing. Earlier reports put second-quarter revenue at over $11.5 billion.

Why it matters

Revenue growth is the main test of whether AI labs can justify their huge valuations and spending.

Why the hype

  • ScaleQuarterly revenue of $16.8 billion would rival large established software firms.

  • MoneyInvestors want proof that AI demand turns into sales.

Background

Anthropic is the San Francisco AI company that makes the Claude models. It was founded in 2021.
