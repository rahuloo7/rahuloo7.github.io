Anthropic shares give back most of their debut gains on day two

Anthropic stock closed about 9% lower on November 20 after a strong debut, as heavy trading turned over more shares than the IPO sold.

By the numbers

$83IPO price
$97.17first-day close
1.26Bshares traded in two days

What happened

Priced at $83, shares opened at $111.50 on November 19 and closed at $97.17. Nasdaq data shows about 1.26 billion shares changed hands in two days, FourWeekMBA reports, and leveraged Anthropic funds are expected to list on November 23.

Why it matters

It is the biggest test yet of how public markets value a frontier AI lab.

Why the hype

  • ScaleThe IPO was described as the largest US listing on record.
  • RivalryBloomberg linked a share drop to reports of Apple building its own AI agent.
  • Ripple effectAsian chip and tech stocks such as SoftBank and TSMC fell the next day.

Background

Anthropic is the San Francisco AI company behind the Claude models. It listed on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.

What to watch next

Watch for the leveraged Anthropic funds' first trading day and the December 15 lock-up release.
