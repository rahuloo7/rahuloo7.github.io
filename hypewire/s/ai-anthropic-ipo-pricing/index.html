Anthropic closes its first trading day up 17% after a record IPO

Anthropic shares opened at $111.50 and closed at $97.17 on their Nasdaq debut, 17% above the $83 IPO price, Forbes reports.

By the numbers

What happened

ANT peaked at $115.10 soon after the open, then fell as low as $93.13 while the wider market slid. It closed with a market value of about $1.27 trillion. The offering raised about $81.3 billion, a US record.

Why it matters

It is the largest IPO on record and the first big public test of how investors value an AI lab.

Why the hype

  • ScaleAbout $81.3 billion was raised, the largest US IPO on record.
  • TimingIt debuted on a day when the Nasdaq 100 and most AI stocks fell.
  • Big nameAmazon and Google are among Anthropic's biggest shareholders.

Background

Anthropic is the San Francisco AI company behind the Claude models.

What to watch next

Watch for how ANT trades in its first full week and whether the broader AI stock slide continues.
