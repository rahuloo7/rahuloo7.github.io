Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share, above its range, and trades today

Anthropic priced its shares at $83, above the $70 to $78 range, for a reported $1.5 trillion valuation; ANT opens on Nasdaq today.

By the numbers

$83IPO price per share
$1.5Tvaluation, Fortune
$81B+raised, CNBC

What happened

Fortune puts the valuation at $1.5 trillion. CNBC reports the offering raised more than $81 billion, a record that tops SpaceX. Orders were reported at close to seven times the 825 million shares first offered, with up to 20% set aside for retail buyers.

Why it matters

It is one of the largest stock market debuts ever and the first big public test of how investors value an AI lab.

Why the hype

  • ScaleCNBC reports more than $81 billion raised, the largest offering on record.
  • Fan demandOrders were reported at close to seven times the shares on offer.
  • Big nameAmazon and Google are set to hold about 15% and 14% of the stock, per a filing.

Background

Anthropic is the San Francisco AI company behind the Claude models.

What to watch next

Watch for the opening trade on Nasdaq and how far it moves from the $83 price.
