Clayface leads the worldwide box office in its second weekend

DC Studios' Clayface topped the global chart with about $23 million, taking its worldwide total to about $83 million.

What happened

Clayface placed second in North America behind You Can See Everything but led worldwide. Its global total reached about $83 million. It is DC Studios' first R-rated horror film.

Why it matters

It is a test of whether smaller, cheaper DC films can work under the new DC Studios plan.

Why the hype

  • MoneyIt has passed its reported $40 million budget.
  • RivalryIt lost the top spot by about $200,000.

Background

Clayface is a Batman villain. The film is part of the DC Universe run by James Gunn and Peter Safran.

What to watch next

Watch for how it holds against Godzilla Minus Zero and The Cat in the Hat this weekend.
