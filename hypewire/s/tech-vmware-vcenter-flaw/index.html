Broadcom patches a critical VMware vCenter flaw rated 9.8 out of 10

Broadcom fixed an authentication bypass in VMware vCenter's directory service, tracked as CVE-2026-123890, with no workaround available.

By the numbers

9.8CVSS severity score

What happened

Fixed versions are out for all supported branches. In its advisory Broadcom said it knew of no attacks and no public exploit code, The Hacker News reports.

Why it matters

vCenter manages the virtual machines that many companies run on, which makes it a prime target.
