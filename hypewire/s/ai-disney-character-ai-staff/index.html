Disney agrees to hire most of Character.AI's workforce

Disney will take on the majority of staff at Character.AI, the chatbot company that filed for bankruptcy this month.

What happened

TechCrunch reports Disney has agreed to hire most of Character.AI's employees. Character.AI filed for bankruptcy earlier in October, about five years after it launched. The rest of the company's assets are being handled in its Chapter 11 case.

Why it matters

It puts a big entertainment company in charge of talent from one of the best-known AI chatbot apps.

Why the hype

  • Big nameDisney owns some of the most valuable characters in entertainment.
  • RiskCharacter.AI's collapse shows how hard AI chatbot apps are to sustain.
  • TrendBig companies keep hiring whole AI teams instead of buying firms outright.

Background

Character.AI lets users chat with AI characters. It is one of the most popular AI companion apps.

What to watch next

Watch for what Disney plans to build with the team and who buys Character.AI's remaining assets.
