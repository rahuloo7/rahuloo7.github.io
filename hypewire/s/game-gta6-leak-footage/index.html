GTA 6 leaker's meme coin crashes as it threatens to post the full game

Cyberleek promised escalating clips as its coin's value rose, then the coin lost half its value in a day.

What happened

The leaker said it would post a full 200GB+ build if its meme coin hit a $30 million market cap, Kotaku reports. The coin passed $4.75 million over the weekend, then fell 50% in 24 hours to about $2.2 million. No playable build has been verified.
