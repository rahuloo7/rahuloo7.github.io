Palo Alto Networks buys AI startup Applied Compute for $4.5 billion in stock

Palo Alto Networks agreed to buy Applied Compute, a year-old startup that runs custom open-weight models on company data, in an all-stock deal.

By the numbers

$4.5Ball-stock deal value
+$1.25Bover its Nov 2 valuation, WSJ

What happened

The WSJ reports the price is $1.25 billion above Applied Compute's valuation from November 2. Palo Alto shares rose about 7% after its earnings, CNBC reports. The deal is subject to adjustments.

Why it matters

Security companies are paying up for AI talent, and this is a big price for a very young company.

Why the hype

  • Money$4.5 billion for a startup that is about one year old.
  • SpeedThe price jumped $1.25 billion in about two weeks.
  • TrendSecurity firms are racing to add AI.

Background

Palo Alto Networks is one of the largest cybersecurity companies in the world.

What to watch next

Watch for regulatory approval and how Palo Alto builds the models into its products.
