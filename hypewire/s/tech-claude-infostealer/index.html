New Windows malware sends stolen data to Claude to rank victims by value

Researchers found an infostealer that feeds stolen personal and financial data to an AI model to judge which victims are worth the most.

What happened

Tom's Hardware reports the malware can switch to another model if one refuses, and keeps stealing data even if the AI step fails. It deletes itself after building its assessment.

Why it matters

It shows criminals building AI into malware, not just using chatbots on the side.

Why the hype

  • RiskAI ranking could help criminals target the most valuable victims.
  • TrendSecurity researchers are finding more malware that calls AI models.

Background

An infostealer is malware that collects passwords, financial details and other data from an infected computer.

What to watch next

Watch for responses from AI companies on detecting this kind of misuse.
