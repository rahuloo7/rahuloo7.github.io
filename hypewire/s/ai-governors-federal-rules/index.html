Bipartisan US governors ask Washington to set rules for the most powerful AI

A bipartisan group of US governors wants federal rules for the most powerful AI systems, while keeping states' power to protect people.

What happened

Bloomberg reports the governors want national rules for frontier systems but say states must still be able to protect consumers, workers and children. Four senators have also proposed a bill on AI disclosure by public companies.

Why it matters

Whether states or Washington write AI rules decides how strict they are across the country.

Why the hype

  • RivalryStates and Washington are both trying to set AI rules.
  • TimingIt comes as AI companies become huge public firms.

Background

Several US states, including California and Colorado, have passed their own AI laws in recent years.

What to watch next

Watch for whether Congress takes up federal rules for frontier AI.
