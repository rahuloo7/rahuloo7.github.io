Anthropic hands its Agent Skills standard to the Agentic AI Foundation

What happened

The foundation announced four new projects at AGNTCon on October 22: Agent Skills, Open Responses, OpenEnv and Alibaba's OpenSandbox. It says Agent Skills has more than 70 integrations and 280 million installs since December 2025.
