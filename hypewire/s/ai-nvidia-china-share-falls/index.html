Nvidia's share of China's AI chip market falls to 34% as Huawei hits 28%

An IDC report says Nvidia's AI accelerator shipments to China fell 44% in the first half of 2026.

By the numbers

34%Nvidia share in China, H1 2026
28%Huawei share
-44%Nvidia China shipments, YoY

What happened

IDC data seen by Reuters shows Nvidia shipped 678,000 AI accelerators in China from January to June, down 44% year over year, Gizmochina reports. Huawei shipped 572,000 for a 28% share. Nine of 11 Chinese chipmakers tracked by IDC grew.

Why it matters

China is one of the biggest AI chip markets, and export rules are pushing it toward local suppliers.

Why the hype

  • RivalryHuawei is now six points behind Nvidia in China.
  • TimingUS export limits and Beijing's self-reliance push both weigh on Nvidia.
  • MoneyJensen Huang has put China's AI chip market at about $50 billion.

Background

Nvidia designs most of the chips used to train AI. Huawei is a Chinese tech group that also makes AI chips.

What to watch next

Watch for whether Huawei overtakes Nvidia in China in the second half of the year.

The take
