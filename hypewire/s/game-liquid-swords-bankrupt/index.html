Liquid Swords, the studio behind Samson, goes bankrupt

The Swedish studio founded by a former Just Cause director is bankrupt after unpaid wages, with nearly 30 jobs affected.

By the numbers

What happened

Breakit reports Liquid Swords was declared bankrupt after failing to pay salaries, affecting nearly 30 staff, who will be compensated by the state. Its game Samson launched in April 2026. A trustee is seeking a buyer.

Why it matters

It adds to a long run of studio closures and job losses across the games industry.
