Microsoft booked $10 billion of revenue from OpenAI in one quarter

Microsoft's quarterly filing shows OpenAI arrangements generated $10 billion, about 11% of its $92.3 billion revenue, TechCrunch reports.

By the numbers

$10BOpenAI-related revenue, quarter
~11%of Microsoft revenue
$7.2BOpenAI receivables

What happened

The figure covers the quarter that ended September 30. Amounts OpenAI owes Microsoft rose to $7.2 billion from $6 billion, according to the filing.

Why it matters

It shows how tied Microsoft's results now are to a single AI partner.

Why the hype

  • Money$10 billion in one quarter from one partner.
  • ScaleThat is about 11% of Microsoft's total revenue.

Background

Microsoft is OpenAI's biggest investor and supplies much of its computing through Azure.

What to watch next

Watch for how the share of revenue from OpenAI changes in coming quarters.
