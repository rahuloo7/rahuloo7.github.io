Next Xbox reportedly due in 2028 at $1,200 to $1,800, with a handheld in 2027

Fortune reports internal projections put Project Helix far above today's console prices unless Microsoft subsidises it.

By the numbers

$1,200-1,800console price, reported
2028Helix target, reported
2027handheld target, reported

What happened

Fortune's profile of Xbox chief Asha Sharma reports Project Helix is due by holiday 2028, with internal price projections of $1,200 to $1,800. It also reports a next-generation handheld by holiday 2027 and plans to sell Game Pass features a la carte.

Why it matters

A console above $1,000 would be a big shift for a market built on cheaper hardware.

Why the hype

  • MoneyThe reported price range is far above any current console.
  • RivalryMicrosoft is trying to revive Xbox against PlayStation and Nintendo.

Background

Xbox is Microsoft's gaming division. Microsoft said in March that Helix will run both Xbox and PC games.

What to watch next

Watch for any official word on pricing or Game Pass changes; none of this is confirmed.
