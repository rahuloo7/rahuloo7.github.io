Apple's trade secret case against OpenAI reaches its first major hearing

Apple accuses OpenAI of using confidential information for its upcoming hardware, and OpenAI has agreed not to use the disputed files for now.

By the numbers

100+files allegedly moved

What happened

Apple's lawsuit alleges OpenAI used confidential Apple information for its hardware project, with more than 100 files said to have been moved. OpenAI agreed not to use the disputed files while the case proceeds, and the suit reached its first major hearing this week.

Why it matters

The case pits two of tech's biggest names against each other as OpenAI moves into devices.
