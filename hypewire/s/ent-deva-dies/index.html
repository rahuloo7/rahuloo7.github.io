Tamil film composer Deva dies at 75

The composer and singer, who scored more than 400 films and pioneered gaana music in cinema, died in Chennai.

By the numbers

75age
400+films scored

What happened

Deva died of sudden cardiac arrest on Saturday at a Chennai hospital, One Mann's Movies reports. He composed for more than 400 films in Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam, including Annaamalai and Baashha. He received full state honours at his burial on Sunday.

Why it matters

Deva's songs shaped decades of South Indian film music, including Rajinikanth classics.

Why the hype

  • Big nameDeva was one of Tamil cinema's best-known composers.
  • NostalgiaHis 1990s songs remain widely loved.
  • ScaleHe scored more than 400 films.

Background

Deva was known as 'Thenisai Thendral' and was famed for bringing Chennai's gaana folk style to film.

Watch for tributes and screenings honouring his work in Tamil cinema.
