Microsoft unveils Maia 300, its own AI chip, to run Copilot from 2027

The third-generation inference chip is built on TSMC's N2P process and is meant to cut Microsoft's reliance on Nvidia.

By the numbers

40%more tokens per MW, claimed
96chips per rack
2027main deployment year

What happened

Microsoft announced Maia 300 on October 12 and said it will become the main system for running AI in Copilot from 2027, at gigawatt scale. It claims 40% more tokens per megawatt than top GPU systems. Each rack holds 96 chips and draws about 167 kW, The Next Web says.

Why it matters

Every big cloud company wants its own AI chips to control costs and supply.

Why the hype

  • RivalryCustom chips let Microsoft buy fewer Nvidia GPUs.
  • ScaleDeployments are planned at gigawatt scale from 2027.
  • MoneyRunning AI models is one of the biggest costs for cloud firms.

Background

Microsoft sells AI tools under the Copilot brand and runs the Azure cloud. Maia is its in-house line of AI chips.

What to watch next

Watch for more detail at Microsoft's Ignite conference in November.
