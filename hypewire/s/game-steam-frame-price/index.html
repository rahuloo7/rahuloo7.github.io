Valve prices the Steam Frame headset from $1,059

Valve's Steam Frame costs $1,059 with 256GB or $1,299 with 1TB, with reservation holders able to order by December or in 2027.

By the numbers

$1,2991TB model

What happened

Gaming Route reports the Steam Frame is priced at $1,059 for 256GB and $1,299 for 1TB. People who reserved one can place orders by December or in 2027, depending on their place in the queue.

Why it matters

Valve's hardware sets the tone for PC VR, and the price is higher than many fans hoped.

Why the hype

  • MoneyA price above $1,000 puts it in premium headset territory.
  • Big nameValve's Steam platform dominates PC gaming.
  • Fan demandPlayers have waited years for new Valve VR hardware.

Background

Valve runs the Steam store and makes hardware such as the Steam Deck and the Index VR headset.

What to watch next

Watch for review units and how quickly the reservation queue moves.
