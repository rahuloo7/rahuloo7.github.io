OpenAI recorder case: prosecutors say a linked chatbot account asked how to build bugs

New details say an account tied to the charged engineer asked about hidden audio recorders days before the device was planted.

By the numbers

Sep 30device found

What happened

Prosecutors say a chatbot account linked to the engineer's wife asked about building and hiding audio recorders in late September, Fox News reports. OpenAI found the device behind a TV in an executive room on September 30. OpenAI says nothing suggests model weights or customer data were taken.

Why it matters

AI labs guard valuable secrets, and an insider bugging executives would be a serious security breach.
