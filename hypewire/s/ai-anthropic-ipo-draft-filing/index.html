Anthropic's draft IPO filing shows $16.3 billion in first-half revenue

A preliminary prospectus reported by the Wall Street Journal shows fast growth, a huge paper loss and vast compute commitments.

By the numbers

$16.3Bfirst-half revenue, reported
$80.7Bfirst-half net loss, reported
~$2TIPO valuation, reported

What happened

The WSJ reports first-half revenue of $16.3 billion, up from $1.1 billion a year earlier, and an $80.7 billion net loss, mostly non-cash. The draft lists about $450 billion in ten-year cloud deals, including $111 billion with Google Cloud.

Why it matters

It is the first detailed look at the finances of a leading AI lab heading for the stock market.

Why the hype

  • ScaleRevenue rose about fifteenfold on the same half a year earlier, per the report.
  • MoneyThe IPO could value Anthropic at about $2 trillion, the WSJ reports.
  • RiskThe draft warns that a shortage of computing power could hold back growth.

Background

Anthropic makes the Claude AI models and is one of the largest AI labs, alongside OpenAI and Google DeepMind.

What to watch next

Watch for a public filing, a listing date and the final valuation, reported to be near $2 trillion.

The take
