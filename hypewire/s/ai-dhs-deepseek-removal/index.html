US homeland security orders DeepSeek removed from federal systems

Federal civilian agencies must remove DeepSeek technology and stop buying its products, the Wall Street Journal reports.

What happened

The Department of Homeland Security has ordered federal civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek technology from their IT systems, the Wall Street Journal reports. The order also blocks future purchases of the company's products and services.

Why it matters

It is a direct US government move against a Chinese AI lab whose models are widely used.

Why the hype

  • RivalryAI has become a central part of US-China technology competition.
  • RiskGovernments worry about where data sent to foreign AI services ends up.
  • TimingIt lands the same day DeepSeek open-sourced its serving software.

Background

DeepSeek is a Chinese AI lab whose low-cost models drew global attention in early 2025.

What to watch next

Watch for whether other governments or US states follow with similar orders.
