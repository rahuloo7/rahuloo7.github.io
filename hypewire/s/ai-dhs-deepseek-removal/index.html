China objects after the US orders DeepSeek out of federal systems

China's commerce ministry called the US ban on DeepSeek in federal civilian agencies discriminatory, NBC News reports.

What happened

Homeland Security has ordered federal civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek technology and stop buying its products. China's Ministry of Commerce called the ban discriminatory and said Beijing will take all necessary measures, without naming any.

Why it matters

It is a direct US government move against a Chinese AI lab whose models are widely used.

Why the hype

  • RivalryAI has become a central part of US-China technology competition.
  • RiskGovernments worry about where data sent to foreign AI services ends up.
  • TimingIt lands the same day DeepSeek open-sourced its serving software.

Background

DeepSeek is a Chinese AI lab whose low-cost models drew global attention in early 2025.

What to watch next

Watch for any specific countermeasures from Beijing and how software vendors handle DeepSeek inside their products.
