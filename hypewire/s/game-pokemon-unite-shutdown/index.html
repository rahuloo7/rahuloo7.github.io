Pokémon Unite will shut down on March 23, 2027

The free-to-play Pokémon battle arena game is ending service, and sales of gems and Unite Club memberships stopped on October 27.

By the numbers

Mar 23, 2027servers close
Nov 12final event
2021launch year

What happened

A final event arrives November 12 with all Unite licences and Holoware released so far, Game Informer reports. A library feature will let players view records after shutdown but must be downloaded before March 23.

Why it matters

Pokémon is one of the biggest game franchises, and Unite was one of its main competitive games.

Why the hype

  • Big namePokémon is one of the best-known game franchises.
  • TimingThe final event lands four months before the servers close.

Background

Pokémon Unite is a five-on-five battle arena game that launched in 2021 on Switch and later on phones.

What to watch next

Watch for the December 5 qualifiers for the Aeos Crown 2027 Tokyo tournament, which go ahead as planned.
