EA Sports FC and Pokemon Go move to a 16 rating in Europe over loot boxes

Ratings body PEGI has started raising games with loot boxes to a 16 rating, VGC reports.

By the numbers

16new PEGI age rating

What happened

PEGI has begun bumping games that contain loot boxes up to a 16 age rating, VGC reports. EA Sports FC and Pokemon Go are among the first big games affected.

Why it matters

Loot boxes have been criticised for years as too close to gambling for children.

Why the hype

  • ControversyCritics have long compared loot boxes to gambling.
  • ScaleBoth games are played by huge numbers of children and adults.

Background

PEGI is the age rating system used across most of Europe.

What to watch next

Watch for which other big games are re-rated and how publishers respond.
