Canal+ seeks 137 million euros from Disney over former subscribers' emails

Canal+ is suing Disney in Paris, alleging it used emails of customers who activated Disney+ through Canal+ to sign them up directly.

By the numbers

€137MCanal+ claim
€7.6MDisney's counterclaim

What happened

The claim covers customers from 2020 to 2024. Disney says those users gave their details to activate its service, and it separately claims 7.6 million euros in unpaid fees. The court declined to merge the two cases.

Why it matters

It is a fight over who owns the customer when streaming bundles end, a common setup across Europe.

Why the hype

  • MoneyCanal+ is seeking 137 million euros.
  • RivalryFormer partners are now fighting over the same subscribers.

Background

Canal+ is France's biggest pay-TV group and bundled Disney+ for several years.

What to watch next

Watch for the Paris commercial court's ruling on either claim.
