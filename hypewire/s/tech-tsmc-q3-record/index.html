TSMC posts record quarter as AI chip demand lifts profit 69%

The world's biggest chipmaker earned $46.7 billion in third-quarter revenue and guided higher for the fourth.

By the numbers

$46.72BQ3 revenue
68.7%net income growth, year on year
$52.3-54.3BQ4 revenue guidance

What happened

TSMC's third-quarter revenue rose 50.9% from a year earlier to $46.72 billion, and net income rose 68.7%, the company said on October 15. Chips at 7nm and below made up 79% of wafer revenue. It guided fourth-quarter revenue to $52.3 to $54.3 billion.

Why it matters

TSMC makes most advanced AI chips, so its results are a direct read on AI demand.

Why the hype

  • ScaleTSMC makes chips for Nvidia, Apple and AMD.
  • MoneyNet profit margin reached 51.1%.
  • TimingIt reported in the middle of a chip stock selloff.

Background

TSMC is a Taiwanese company that manufactures chips designed by other firms.

What to watch next

Watch for whether the strong guidance steadies chip stocks after this week's selloff.
