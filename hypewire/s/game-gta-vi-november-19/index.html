GTA VI pre-loads open November 10 and the game needs 212GB

Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto VI can be pre-loaded from November 10 ahead of its November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

By the numbers

Nov 19launch date
212GBinstall size
Nov 10digital pre-load opens

What happened

Kotaku reports a 212GB install, about a third of a base PS5's storage. Xbox pre-loads start at 7 p.m. ET on November 9, and boxed codes can pre-load from November 12. New Zealand gets it first, on November 18 UTC.

Why it matters

It is the most anticipated game in years and will dominate the holiday season.

Why the hype

  • Fan demandFans have waited more than a decade for a new mainline GTA.
  • ScaleIts 212GB install will fill a big part of many consoles.
  • TimingIt launches right before the holiday shopping season.

Background

Rockstar Games makes Grand Theft Auto. GTA V, from 2013, is one of the best-selling games ever.

What to watch next

Watch for review timing and launch-day server load as release nears.
