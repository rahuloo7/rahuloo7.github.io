Anthropic files its S-1 and plans a Nasdaq listing as ANT

Anthropic made its IPO filing public, showing $16.3 billion in first-half revenue and its first quarterly operating profit.

By the numbers

$16.3Bfirst-half 2026 revenue
$974MQ2 operating income
$80.7Bfirst-half net loss

What happened

The S-1, filed October 26, shows first-half revenue of about $16.3 billion against $1.16 billion a year earlier, and an $80.7 billion net loss, mostly noncash. About 1,900 customers pay at least $1 million a year, TechStartups reports.

Why it matters

It could be one of the largest stock listings ever and the first public look at a frontier AI lab's books.

Why the hype

  • ScaleWall Street talk puts a listing valuation at about $1.5 trillion to $2 trillion.
  • MoneyFirst-half revenue of $16.3 billion compares with $4.6 billion for all of 2025.
  • RivalryOpenAI is not expected to list until early 2027, Yahoo Finance says.

Background

Anthropic makes the Claude AI models. It confirmed in June that it had confidentially filed a draft S-1 with the SEC.

What to watch next

Watch for the price range, the share count and the roadshow schedule.
