Anthropic is reported to be days from making its IPO filing public

Anthropic could publish its S-1 as soon as October 23, reports say, opening the way to a possible November listing.

By the numbers

$16.3Bfirst-half 2026 revenue
$16.8BQ3 revenue, told to investors
$80B+first-half net loss

What happened

Draft papers seen by the WSJ and Reuters show $16.3 billion in first-half revenue and a net loss above $80 billion, mostly a $72 billion non-cash charge. Anthropic told investors Q3 revenue reached $16.8 billion.

Why it matters

It would be one of the largest stock market debuts ever and a major test of investor appetite for AI.

Why the hype

  • ScaleWall Street expects a raise of up to $100 billion, The Wall Street Journal reports.
  • MoneyReports put about $450 billion of cloud commitments on Anthropic's books over 7 to 10 years.
  • RivalryOpenAI is raising privately at the same time and is also reported to be eyeing an IPO.

Background

Anthropic makes the Claude AI models. It submitted a confidential draft IPO filing to the SEC in June.

What to watch next

Watch for the public S-1, the size of the offering and the valuation Anthropic seeks.

The take
