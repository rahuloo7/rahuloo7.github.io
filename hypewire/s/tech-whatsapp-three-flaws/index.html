WhatsApp patches three flaws, including one it suspects was used in scams

Meta's WhatsApp fixed three security flaws, including an Android bug in ad-reply links that it suspects was used in scam messages.

By the numbers

3flaws patched
2.26.17.4fixed Android version

What happened

The Android bug, CVE-2026-23871, affects WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business for Android before version 2.26.17.4, The Hacker News reports.

Why it matters

WhatsApp is used by billions of people, so a flaw used in scams reaches a huge number of phones.

Why the hype

  • ScaleWhatsApp is one of the world's most used apps.
  • RiskThe bug is suspected of being used in scams.

Background

WhatsApp is a messaging app owned by Meta. Updating the app from the store installs the fix.

What to watch next

Watch for the update reaching all users and any details on the scams that used the bug.
