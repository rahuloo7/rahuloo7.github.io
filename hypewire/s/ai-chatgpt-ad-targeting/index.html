ChatGPT ads can target users by email, phone number and prompts, report says

404 Media reports advertisers on ChatGPT can reach people using emails, phone numbers and what they type into the chatbot.

By the numbers

~1BChatGPT monthly users

What happened

404 Media reported on October 15 that advertisers can target ChatGPT users by email address, phone number or device identifier. Targeting is also said to draw on users' prompts. ChatGPT has about one billion monthly users.

Why it matters

Ads based on private chats raise new privacy questions for a service used by about a billion people.

Why the hype

  • ControversyPeople often tell chatbots things they would not post publicly.
  • ScaleChatGPT reaches about a billion people a month.
  • MoneyAds are a new revenue source for OpenAI.

Background

OpenAI began showing ads in ChatGPT this year, joining Google and Meta in advertising.

What to watch next

Watch for OpenAI's response and any interest from privacy regulators.
