Google Cloud revenue doubles but Alphabet shares fall after hours

Alphabet's third-quarter revenue rose 25% to $127.6 billion as Google Cloud grew 103%, yet shares fell about 9% after hours.

By the numbers

$30.7BGoogle Cloud revenue, +103%
$51.4Bquarterly capex
-9.4%after-hours share move

What happened

Capital spending more than doubled to $51.4 billion and search revenue growth slowed to 12%, StockTitan reports. Earnings per share fell 26% to $2.12, hit by an $8.7 billion loss on equity stakes.

Why it matters

Investors are weighing fast AI-driven cloud growth against slower search and soaring spending.

Why the hype

  • MoneyCapex hit $51.4 billion in a single quarter.
  • TrendCloud supplied an estimated 62% of the revenue Alphabet added in a year.
  • RiskSearch growth slowed to 12% from 17% in the prior quarter.

Background

Alphabet is Google's parent company. Google Cloud rents computing, including its own TPU chips, to businesses.

What to watch next

Watch for any change to Alphabet's $195 billion to $205 billion capex guidance for 2026.
