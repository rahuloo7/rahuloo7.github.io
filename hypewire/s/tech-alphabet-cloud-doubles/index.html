Alphabet loses a record sum of market value despite Google Cloud doubling

Alphabet shares fell more than 12% on Wednesday after results, erasing about $500 billion as negative free cash flow worried investors.

By the numbers

$30.7BGoogle Cloud revenue, +103%
-12%share fall on Wednesday
$924.4Bpurchase commitments

What happened

Revenue rose 25% to $127.6 billion and Google Cloud grew 103% to $30.7 billion. Capex hit $51.4 billion. Alphabet's 10-Q lists $924.4 billion in purchase commitments, TheNextWeb reports.

Why it matters

Investors are weighing fast AI-driven cloud growth against soaring spending.

Why the hype

  • MoneyCapex hit $51.4 billion in a single quarter.
  • RiskFree cash flow turned negative, the FT reports.
  • ScaleThe fall erased a record sum of market value.

Background

Alphabet is Google's parent company. Google Cloud rents computing, including its own TPU chips, to businesses.

What to watch next

Watch for any change to Alphabet's $195 billion to $205 billion capex guidance for 2026.
