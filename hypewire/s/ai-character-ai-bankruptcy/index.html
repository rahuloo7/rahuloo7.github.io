Character.ai files for bankruptcy as Disney lines up a $20 million deal

The AI companion startup filed for Chapter 11 protection, citing legal and operational pressures.

By the numbers

$20MDisney deal
Chapter 11bankruptcy filing

What happened

Character.ai filed for Chapter 11 and agreed a $20 million deal with Disney for a non-exclusive, perpetual license, CBS News reports. A court-supervised sale is expected by late November or early December, and the service keeps running for now. It faces lawsuits from Kentucky and Pennsylvania.

Why it matters

Character.ai was one of the best-known chatbot startups, and its fall shows the cost of AI safety lawsuits.

Why the hype

  • Big nameDisney is the lead bidder for the company's assets.
  • RiskLawsuits over harm to young users weighed on the company.
  • TrendAI companion apps face growing legal scrutiny.

Background

Character.ai lets users chat with AI characters. Google struck a licensing deal with the company in 2024.

What to watch next

Watch for rival bids in the court-supervised sale and what happens to the app's users.
