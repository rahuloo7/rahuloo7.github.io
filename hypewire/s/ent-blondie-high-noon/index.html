Blondie announce High Noon, their last album with drummer Clem Burke

The band's first album since 2017 arrives February 26, 2027, led by the single Spectral Eyes with Johnny Marr.

By the numbers

Feb 26, 2027release date
2017previous album

What happened

High Noon is out on BMG on February 26, 2027, Stereogum reports, produced by John Congleton. Johnny Marr wrote and plays on Spectral Eyes. Chris Stein said Clem Burke, who died in 2025, played the whole record.

Why it matters

It is Blondie's first new music in years and a farewell to a founding-era drummer.

Why the hype

  • NostalgiaBlondie has been active since the 1970s.
  • Big nameJohnny Marr of The Smiths features on the lead single.
  • TimingIt is the band's first album since Pollinator in 2017.

Background

Blondie, fronted by Debbie Harry, rose from the New York punk scene with hits such as Heart of Glass.
