Hackers heat supermarket fridges across France, spoiling food

Intermarché, Carrefour, Super U and Monoprix stores were hit through outdated Danfoss refrigeration controllers.

By the numbers

150Intermarché stores hit
80Carrefour stores hit
3,726controllers found online in Aug

What happened

Attackers remotely forced defrost cycles at refrigeration systems in French supermarkets from October 11, Infosecurity Magazine reports. Intermarché said 150 stores were hit and Carrefour 80. Danfoss said affected units ran obsolete or un-updated controllers. Belgium and the Netherlands were reportedly affected.

Why it matters

It is a rare attack where hacking caused direct physical damage in everyday shops.

Why the hype

  • NoveltyRemotely heating supermarket fridges is a new kind of attack.
  • ScaleHundreds of stores across several chains were affected.
  • RiskResearchers found thousands of these controllers exposed online.

Background

Danfoss is a Danish maker of refrigeration and heating equipment used in shops worldwide.

What to watch next

Watch for findings from France's cybersecurity agency and whether attackers are identified.
