Safari beta hints at Apple's first touchscreen MacBook ahead of a reported event

New interface elements in Safari suggest macOS 27 is being readied for touch, as Apple is reported to plan a late-October showcase.

By the numbers

Oct 27-28showcase dates, reported

What happened

Macworld reports new UI elements in the latest Safari beta that point to touch support in macOS 27. Bloomberg's Mark Gurman earlier reported Apple plans to show a touchscreen MacBook Pro at a showcase around October 27 to 28.

Why it matters

A touchscreen Mac would reverse years of Apple saying touch did not belong on laptops.

Why the hype

  • First of its kindIt would be the first Mac with a touchscreen.
  • TimingThe showcase is reported for the end of October.

Background

Apple has kept touch to the iPhone and iPad, while many Windows laptops have touchscreens.

What to watch next

Watch for Apple confirming an event date.
