US chip curbs stall billions in Asian data centre loans

Lenders are holding back data centre financing tied to ByteDance, Alibaba and Tencent after new US chip curbs, Bloomberg reports.

By the numbers

$6.6Bdelayed loan, reported

What happened

Bloomberg, citing sources, says some lenders are delaying a $6.6 billion loan for Bridge Data Centres. It also reports Asian operators are courting US clients as rental prices for B300 chips fall.

Why it matters

Data centres in Asia run on loans and Nvidia chips, so tighter rules can freeze projects quickly.

Why the hype

  • MoneyA single delayed loan is $6.6 billion.
  • PoliticsThe pause follows new US export rules.

Background

ByteDance owns TikTok; Alibaba and Tencent are two of China's largest tech companies.

What to watch next

Watch for whether the loans are restructured or cancelled.
