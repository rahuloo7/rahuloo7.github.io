Banks, airlines and retailers are locking AI agents out of their sites

Amazon, Delta and major US banks are blocking outside AI agents such as Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots from acting for users.

What happened

Reports say Amazon removed Meta's Muse from its store, Delta blocks outside agents from booking, and big banks keep bots out of accounts. Schwab's chief said it blocks dozens to hundreds of agent attempts in weekly reports.

Why it matters

AI agents are pitched as doing errands for you, which only works if websites let them in.

Why the hype

  • RivalrySites want to keep customers on their own apps and ads.
  • RiskBanks cite account security and fraud.
  • MoneySponsored listings and ad revenue are at stake.

Background

AI agents are chatbots that can take actions such as shopping or booking on a user's behalf.

What to watch next

Watch for paid deals between AI companies and the sites now blocking them.

The take
