Resident Evil holds No. 1 in China and passes $361 million worldwide

The film led China's box office for a second weekend with $9.3 million, as the country's yearly takings run 29% behind 2025.

By the numbers

$361.2Mworldwide total
$9.3MChina weekend
-29.2%China box office vs 2025

What happened

Resident Evil took RMB 62.5 million ($9.3M) in China, for $55.8M there since October 5, One Mann's Movies reports. It was third worldwide on the weekend with $22.6M. China's box office is $4.67B for the year, down 29.2% on 2025.

Why it matters

It is one of the year's biggest video game films, and China is a key market for Hollywood.
