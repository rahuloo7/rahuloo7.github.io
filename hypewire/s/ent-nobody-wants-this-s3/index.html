Nobody Wants This season 3 slips to No. 2 with 7.2 million views

The romantic comedy drew 7.2 million views in its second week, behind the new Matthew Perry documentary.

By the numbers

7.2Mviews, week 2
13.4Mviews over two weeks

What happened

What's on Netflix says season 3 has 13.4 million views over two weeks, against 26.2 million for season 1 and 18 million for season 2 over the same span. Its hours rose about 17% in week two.

Why it matters

A comedy holding the top spot in its third season is rare for a streaming show.

Why the hype

  • Fan demandIts first two seasons built a loyal audience.
  • TimingIt arrived in a week with few big new series.

Background

Nobody Wants This is a Netflix romantic comedy series that first premiered in 2024.

What to watch next

Watch for whether Netflix renews it for a fourth season.
