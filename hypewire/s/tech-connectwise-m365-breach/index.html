Stolen ConnectWise credential used to sign in to 173 Microsoft 365 tenants

A compromised account for ConnectWise Cloud Backup was used to access Microsoft 365 tenants belonging to partners and customers.

By the numbers

173Microsoft 365 tenants accessed

What happened

The Hacker News reports a compromised Microsoft 365 credential tied to ConnectWise Cloud Backup, formerly SkyKick, was used for unauthorized sign-ins across 173 partner and customer tenants.

Why it matters

One stolen key reaching many companies is the supply chain risk security teams fear most.

Why the hype

  • ScaleOne credential opened doors at 173 organizations.
  • RiskIT service tools have wide access to client systems.

Background

ConnectWise makes software IT service providers use to manage and back up their clients' systems.

What to watch next

Watch for ConnectWise's findings on what data was accessed.
