Anthropic posts a Claude-authored paper claiming a major result in complexity theory

Anthropic published a 70-page paper credited to Claude that claims VP is not equal to VNP, with a Lean formalisation; no human has checked it yet.

By the numbers

70pages in the paper

What happened

The paper and proof code were pushed to Anthropic's formal-math repository on November 23. Anthropic says no human mathematician has checked it. It explicitly does not settle P vs NP.

Why it matters

It comes days after the Riemann claim, adding to a debate about AI doing frontier mathematics.

Why the hype

  • First of its kindA paper with an AI as its author line is unusual.

Background

VP and VNP are algebraic versions of the famous P and NP classes, which ask which problems are easy to compute.
