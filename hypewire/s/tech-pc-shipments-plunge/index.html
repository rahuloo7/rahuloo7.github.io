Global PC shipments fall 21% as memory and chip prices climb

Counterpoint says PC shipments dropped 21% year on year in Q3 and expects a 27% fall in Q4.

By the numbers

-21%Q3 PC shipments, year on year
-27%Q4 forecast

What happened

Higher prices for RAM, SSDs and processors drove the drop, which ended more than two years of growth, Dataconomy reports. Some PC makers are reviving older DDR4 products to limit price rises. Counterpoint expects 2027 to be broadly flat.

Why it matters

Rising component costs mean higher prices and fewer cheap laptops for buyers this holiday season.

Why the hype

  • ScaleA 21% fall is one of the steepest quarterly drops in years.
  • MoneyMemory, storage and chip prices have all risen.
  • RiskCounterpoint expects the decline to deepen to 27% in Q4.

Background

Counterpoint Research is a market analysis firm that tracks phone and PC sales.

What to watch next

Watch for PC makers' quarterly results and holiday pricing.

The take
