Castlevania: Belmont's Curse launches today after strong reviews

Konami's new 2D Castlevania arrives on October 15 for $29.99, with an 86 Metacritic score reported before launch.

By the numbers

86Metacritic score, reported
32/40Famitsu score
$29.99price

What happened

Castlevania: Belmont's Curse, from the Dead Cells team at Evil Empire and Motion Twin, launches October 15 at $29.99, Nintendo Life reports. Famitsu's four critics gave it 32 out of 40. Pure Xbox earlier reported scores of 86 on Metacritic and 85 on OpenCritic.

Why it matters

It is the first new Castlevania game in years, and fans have waited a long time for one this well received.

Why the hype

  • NostalgiaCastlevania is a decades-old Konami series.

Background

Castlevania is Konami's long-running action series about the Belmont family's fight with Dracula.
