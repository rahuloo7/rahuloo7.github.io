Godzilla Minus Zero is tracking for a $30 million US opening

Toho's sequel to Godzilla Minus One is projected to open around $30 million in North America this weekend, ahead of The Cat in the Hat.

By the numbers

~$30Mopening weekend, projected
3,254North American screens
$11.4MMinus One's US opening

What happened

Variety puts it on 3,254 screens, the first Japanese production filmed for IMAX. Minus One opened to $11.4 million. TheWrap says a win would make it only the fourth non-English film to top the US box office.

Why it matters

Minus One was a surprise global hit and an Oscar winner, so the sequel carries big expectations.

Why the hype

  • RivalryIt faces Warner Bros.' animated The Cat in the Hat on the same weekend.
  • First of its kindIt is the first Japanese production filmed for IMAX.
  • Fan demandGodzilla Minus One won an Oscar for visual effects and built a big US following.

Background

Toho is the Japanese studio that created Godzilla in 1954.

What to watch next

Watch for Friday's numbers and whether it beats The Cat in the Hat for first place.
