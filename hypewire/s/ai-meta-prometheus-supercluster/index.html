Meta details Prometheus, its gigawatt-scale AI supercluster in Ohio

Meta's engineers say the New Albany site links hundreds of thousands of GPUs, aiming for about a million to train Muse models.

What happened

In an engineering post, Meta said phase one of Prometheus connects hundreds of thousands of GPUs, with a target of about one million for Muse training. It reports more than 85% pre-training goodput and a 99% cut in the interruption rate.

Why it matters

Meta is spending heavily on AI computing, and its earnings this week will put that spending in focus.

Why the hype

  • ScaleA million GPUs would be among the largest AI clusters announced.

Background

Meta owns Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp and trains its own AI models, now under the Muse name.

What to watch next
