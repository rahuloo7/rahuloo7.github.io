Signal blocks mobile screenshots of chats to keep out AI assistants

Signal is restricting screenshots on mobile and blocking assistants such as Siri and Gemini from capturing chats, calling AI agents a threat to privacy.

What happened

TechCrunch reports Signal described AI agents as an existential threat to privacy. BleepingComputer reports the block will cover Siri and Gemini on iOS and Android.

Why it matters

AI assistants that read screens could see messages people expect to stay private.

Why the hype

  • RiskAssistants that read screens could expose private chats.
  • RivalryIt puts Signal at odds with Apple's and Google's assistants.

Background

Signal is an encrypted messaging app run by a non-profit and known for its focus on privacy.

What to watch next

Watch for how Apple and Google respond and whether other messaging apps follow.
