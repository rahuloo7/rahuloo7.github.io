Castlevania: Belmont's Curse debuts third in the UK charts

Konami's new Castlevania entered at No. 3 behind EA Sports FC 27 and newcomer Control Resonant.

By the numbers

No. 3UK chart debut
$29.99US price
50%UK sales on PS5

What happened

EA Sports FC 27 held No. 1, Control Resonant debuted at No. 2 and Belmont's Curse at No. 3, Nintendo Life reports from GfK data. Half of Castlevania's UK sales were on PS5 and 45% on Switch. It launched October 15 at $29.99 in the US.

Why it matters

It is the first new Castlevania in years, and an early chart showing tests whether fans came back.

Why the hype

  • NostalgiaCastlevania is one of gaming's oldest series.

Background

Castlevania is Konami's vampire-hunting series, first released in 1986.
