Jailer 2 passes ₹800 crore to become the top-grossing Tamil film

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has taken about ₹807 crore worldwide in 18 days, passing his own 2.0 as the biggest Tamil film ever.

By the numbers

₹807 crworldwide gross, 18 days
₹533.75 crIndia gross
₹273.5 croverseas gross

What happened

Sacnilk figures cited by The Hollywood Reporter India put its India gross at ₹533.75 crore and overseas at ₹273.50 crore. It crossed ₹800 crore on its third Sunday. PVR INOX and Cinepolis did not screen the Hindi version.

Why it matters

Rajinikanth is one of India's biggest stars, and records like this are closely followed.

Why the hype

  • Big nameRajinikanth has led Tamil cinema for decades.
  • RivalryIt is chasing 2.0, another Rajinikanth film.
  • ScaleAbout $81 million worldwide in 16 days.

Background

Jailer 2 is the sequel to the 2023 hit Jailer. Tamil cinema is based in Chennai in southern India.
