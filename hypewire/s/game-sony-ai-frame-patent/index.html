Sony patent describes AI that predicts game frames to cut lag

A newly published Sony patent covers predicting upcoming frames without waiting for the real ones to render.

US patent application 2026/0320740, published October 15, describes using AI to predict the next frame of a game before it is rendered, Patentlyze reports. The filing does not mention the PS6.
