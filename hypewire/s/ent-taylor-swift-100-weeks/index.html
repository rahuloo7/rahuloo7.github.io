Taylor Swift becomes the first solo act with 100 weeks at No. 1

The Life of a Showgirl spent a 14th week atop the Billboard 200, leaving only the Beatles ahead of her on the all-time list.

By the numbers

100weeks at No. 1, all albums
93Kunits this week
132weeks for the Beatles

What happened

The Life of a Showgirl earned 93,000 units to lead the Billboard 200 for a 14th week, Billboard reports. That gives Swift 100 weeks at No. 1 across 15 albums, behind the Beatles' 132. The Beatles' Rubber Soul also returned to No. 4.

Why it matters

It is a rare chart record, and only one act in history has more weeks at the top.

Why the hype

  • Big nameSwift is one of the best-selling artists in the world.
  • RecordNo solo act had reached 100 weeks at No. 1 before.
  • Fan demandAn expanded Encore edition added four new songs.

Background

The Billboard 200 ranks the most popular albums in the US each week, combining sales and streams.

What to watch next

Watch for whether the album extends its run on next week's chart.
