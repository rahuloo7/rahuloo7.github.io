Nvidia shows a data center that ships in containers at GTC Berlin

Nvidia's DSX POD packs its Vera Rubin design into containers it says cut deployment to about 14 days.

By the numbers

~14 daysdeployment time, Nvidia says
Early 2027first deliveries
6suppliers expanding

What happened

At GTC Berlin on October 21, Nvidia launched DSX POD, a containerised Vera Rubin design, with first deliveries slated for early 2027. It also named six suppliers, including Ibiden and Amkor, to expand substrate and packaging capacity.

Why it matters

Getting AI data centers built faster is one of the biggest bottlenecks in the industry.

Why the hype

  • ScaleNvidia says deployment drops from about six months to 14 days.
  • Big nameNvidia sets the pace for nearly every AI data center build.

Background

Nvidia makes most of the chips used to train and run AI models.

What to watch next

Watch for the first DSX POD customers and whether the 14-day claim holds in practice.
