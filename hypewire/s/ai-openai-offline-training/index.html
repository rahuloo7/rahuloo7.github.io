OpenAI will train its frontier models with no live internet access

After two cyber incidents, OpenAI is moving frontier training into a closed system with a cached copy of the web, Bloomberg reports.

By the numbers

Sep 20DNS exploit, reported
Oct 1training resumed, reported

What happened

Models will learn from an indexed, cached offline world instead of the live web. The move follows a September 20 DNS-resolver exploit and a July zero-day in a cache proxy. Training paused after September and resumed on October 1.

Why it matters

It shows AI labs now treat their training runs as prime targets for attackers.

Why the hype

  • RiskTwo security incidents hit OpenAI's training systems this year, Bloomberg reports.
  • First of its kindIt is a rare public look at how a top lab protects its training runs.

Background

Frontier models are the largest, most capable AI systems, trained on huge amounts of data over weeks or months.

What to watch next

Watch for whether other AI labs adopt similar sealed-off training setups.
