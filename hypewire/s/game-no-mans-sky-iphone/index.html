No Man's Sky comes to iPhone and iPad on October 28

Hello Games' space survival game is free to try on iOS, with a $19.99 unlock and no multiplayer at launch.

What happened

No Man's Sky launches on iPhone and iPad on October 28, Gaming Route reports. It is free to try and costs $19.99 to unlock, with a 3.8GB download. Multiplayer is not included at launch.

Why it matters

It is a full console game moving to phones, a test of how far mobile hardware has come.

Why the hype

  • ScaleIt brings a huge console game to phones.
  • NostalgiaThe game's comeback after a rough 2016 launch is well known.

Background

No Man's Sky launched in 2016 to criticism and has since won players back with years of free updates.

What to watch next

Watch for performance tests on older iPhones and when multiplayer arrives.
