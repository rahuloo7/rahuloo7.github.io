Konami's president says AI cannot replace the people behind great games

Hideki Hayakawa says generative AI will not change the games market much and that creators matter most.

What happened

In Konami's new business report, Hayakawa said teams create new ideas through a hard-to-replace 'chemical reaction', VGC reports. He said Konami still helps creators learn new tools and use them safely.
