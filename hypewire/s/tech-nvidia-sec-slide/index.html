Nvidia falls 13% after a report of an SEC look at its accounting

Nvidia shares fell 13% on November 20 after Reuters reported SEC enforcement staff are examining possible accounting issues at the company.

By the numbers

-13%share fall, November 20
~$899Bvalue lost in a week, FourWeekMBA
$125.53close, November 20

What happened

FourWeekMBA calculates Nvidia lost about $899 billion in market value over the week, closing at $125.53. The SEC inquiry has not been confirmed by the agency, and the company has not been accused of wrongdoing.

Why it matters

Nvidia is the most important chip supplier for AI, so trouble there shakes the whole tech market.

Why the hype

  • ScaleAbout $899 billion in value fell away in a week.
  • RiskSeveral regulators are now looking at the company.
  • TimingIt came weeks after its long-time CEO resigned.

Background

Nvidia makes the GPUs that train and run most large AI models. Jensen Huang resigned as CEO on November 12.

What to watch next

Watch for Nvidia's response and the congressional interviews scheduled for December 1 and 2.
